# The Release Train

At the first station, scope is printed clean,
A client names the work, the edge, the need.
No foggy promise boards the morning screen,
Only tasks with tracks that reviewers can read.

At the second station, evidence takes its seat,
A diff, a test, a note on what was changed.
The platform checks the ticket and receipt,
Then keeps each handoff ordered and arranged.

At the third station, escrow guards the fare,
Not as a vault of doubt, but measured trust.
The builder ships with proof and patient care,
The client signs when outcomes match the must.

At the final station, payout leaves the rail,
A quiet signal turning green at last.
FreelanceFlow turns delivered work from trail
To settled value, logged and moving past.
